Our story

A Cornerstone of Athens Since 1904

With roots dating back to 1904, Flanagan Lumber Company has been serving builders, contractors, and DIY enthusiasts for more than 120 years. What started as a small local lumberyard has grown into a full-service building materials supplier — without ever losing its hometown feel.

Today, our third-generation owner keeps the legacy going the only way we know how: quality products, honest prices, and service with some manners to it. When you walk through our doors, you're greeted by people who know lumber, know building, and know your name.
