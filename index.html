Our Story

A Cornerstone of the Athens Community Since 1904

With roots dating back to 1904, Flanagan Lumber Company has been serving builders, contractors, and DIY enthusiasts for more than 120 years. What started as a small local lumberyard has grown into a full-service building materials supplier — without ever losing its hometown feel.

Today, our third-generation store owner ensures the legacy of exceptional customer service and quality products lives on. When you walk through our doors, you're greeted by people who know lumber, know building, and know your name.
